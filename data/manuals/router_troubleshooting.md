# VisionCare Demo Router Troubleshooting

## Red Status Light

A steady or blinking red status light means the router did not complete its network health check. The most common causes are a failed WAN connection, a loose cable, or a firmware boot fault.

Recommended first response:

1. Confirm that the power LED is stable.
2. Confirm that the WAN or internet cable is firmly seated.
3. Power cycle the router once before attempting factory reset.

## Power Cycle Steps

Use this procedure for red-light, boot-loop, and intermittent connectivity issues.

1. Unplug the router power adapter.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Reconnect power.
4. Wait up to 3 minutes for boot completion.
5. Check whether the status light returns to green or white.

## Cable Checks

Use this procedure when the issue may involve WAN, LAN, or power cable seating.

1. Check the WAN cable at both the router and modem ends.
2. Inspect the power adapter for visible damage.
3. Replace visibly frayed or bent cables before further testing.
4. Confirm that the modem or upstream device is powered on.

## Factory Reset Warning

Factory reset erases Wi-Fi name, password, and local configuration. Only suggest factory reset after power cycle and cable checks fail, and warn the customer before continuing.

## Escalation Conditions

Escalate to hardware support when:

1. The red status light remains after power cycle and cable checks.
2. The customer reports smoke, heat, water damage, or electrical smell.
3. The router does not power on with a known-good adapter.
4. The model number cannot be identified confidently.
