Phase 6 completed:
- Added backend/agent.py with AgentSessionState, SupportAgent, and a one-turn run_support_workflow convenience entry point.
- Connected local document sanitization, sanitized model analysis, strict model-number product identification, focused manual loading, warranty checks, RMA creation, human escalation, and JSONL telemetry.
- Local OCR product fields take precedence over model-extracted document fields, and failed redaction verification blocks document model calls.
- The agent owns policy decisions; model output supplies observations but cannot bypass warranty eligibility or directly create an RMA.
- Stable session-based idempotency keys prevent duplicate RMAs and escalation tickets.
- Expired warranties never call initiate_rma_process and receive an out-of-warranty response with human-support options.
- Escalation triggers cover highly negative sentiment, model confidence below the configured threshold, explicit human requests, and two failed diagnosis attempts.
- Highly negative sentiment intentionally preempts automated warranty/RMA handling and routes the session to human support.
- Product-name-only observations do not select a manual; the agent asks for the model number and escalates after the second failed attempt.
- Privacy redaction applies to uploaded documents, not arbitrary product photos; incidental PII visible in a product image is out of scope for v1.
- Text fallback serial parsing accepts the documented dashed or spaced fixture format, not separator-free serials.
- Removed a no-op serial normalization replacement from backend/agent.py.
- Tool telemetry excludes raw serial numbers, user identifiers, messages, and document contents.
- Session summaries are appended once, only after a terminal outcome is known, so an open troubleshooting turn can continue without recording a premature result.
- Added tests/test_agent.py with valid and expired warranty flows, RMA and escalation idempotency, strict product routing, low-confidence and highly-negative-sentiment escalation, privacy failure blocking, multi-turn summary behavior, and a real OCR/redaction integration test.
